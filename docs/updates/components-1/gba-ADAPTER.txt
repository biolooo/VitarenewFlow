Build: devkitPro/devkitarm wie BUILD.txt der Library.
cmake -S . -B build-3ds
  -DCMAKE_TOOLCHAIN_FILE=src/platform/3ds/CMakeToolchain.txt
  -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release -DBUILD_QT=OFF -DBUILD_SDL=OFF
  -DBUILD_SHARED=OFF -DBUILD_STATIC=ON -DBUILD_TEST=OFF
cmake --build build-3ds --target mgba.elf -j4
smdhtool/3dsxtool erzeugen die separat benannte mgba-library.3dsx.
